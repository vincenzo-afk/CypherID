# Problem Statement

## SIH26125 — Official Statement
Design and develop a blockchain-based secure platform for identity, access control, and digital asset management for Bharat Electronics Limited.

## Extended Problem Analysis

### Centralized IAM Failures
- Single points of failure for identity data
- No cryptographic proof of access decisions
- Opaque audit logs susceptible to tampering
- Password-based authentication vulnerable to breach
- No user ownership of identity credentials

### Digital Asset Risks
- Document ownership not cryptographically verifiable
- No tamper-evident provenance chain
- Unauthorized transfer of sensitive materials

### Content Protection Gap
- Confidential documents displayed on screen are vulnerable to camera capture
- Exam content can be photographed during online examinations
- Training videos can be screen-recorded without authorization
- Existing web DRM solutions do not address physical camera capture

### Insider Threat
- Access patterns not monitored in real-time
- Anomalous behavior not flagged automatically
- No on-chain security alert record
