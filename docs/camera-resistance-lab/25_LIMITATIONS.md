# Lab Limitations

## Measurement Limitations
1. Human readability scores are subjective (mitigated by panel averaging and blinding)
2. OCR accuracy depends on OCR tool version and configuration (must be documented)
3. Physical camera tests are highly environment-sensitive; results not universally generalizable
4. Lab results may not represent all real-world capture scenarios

## Scope Limitations
1. Lab does not test against all camera models (practical impossibility)
2. Lab does not test against all screen recording software
3. Lab does not test against GPU-level capture tools
4. Lab does not simulate adversarial post-processing (image stacking, super-resolution)

## Result Validity
Results are valid for the specific configurations tested.
Do not extrapolate lab results to claim universal effectiveness.

## Correct Use of Lab Results
- Tune protection profile parameters
- Identify which techniques provide measurable benefit
- Communicate measured (not theoretical) effectiveness in documentation
- Identify conditions where protection is ineffective (document honestly)

## Incorrect Use of Lab Results
- Claiming universal protection based on favorable lab conditions
- Omitting unfavorable results from reports
- Fabricating results for uncovered configurations
