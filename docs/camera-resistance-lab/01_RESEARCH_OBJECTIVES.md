# Research Objectives

## Primary Research Question
Which rendering technique configurations, under which physical and digital capture conditions, most effectively reduce camera readability of protected content while maintaining human readability?

## Secondary Research Questions
1. What is the measurable impact of each technique on camera readability?
2. What is the measurable impact on human readability?
3. How do results vary by camera device, frame rate, and exposure?
4. How do results vary by display refresh rate and viewing distance?
5. What is the OCR recovery rate from captured images?
6. What is the performance cost of each technique?

## Research Approach
Empirical measurement only. No fabricated or estimated results.
All claims based on measured results with documented methodology.

## Scope
This lab evaluates the PROTECTION subsystem's rendering techniques.
It does not evaluate other security layers (authentication, authorization, encryption).

## Output
Measured results in `docs/reports/CAMERA_RESISTANCE_REPORT.md`.
Results inform: profile parameter tuning, technique selection, marketing claims.
