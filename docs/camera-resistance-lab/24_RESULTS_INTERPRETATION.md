# Results Interpretation

Interpreting OCR accuracy:
- > 0.70: content substantially readable by camera
- 0.30–0.70: partial readability, significant degradation
- < 0.30: low readability, strong camera resistance
- < 0.10: very low readability

Note: adversary with post-processing may do better than Tesseract defaults.
Always note: this is measured under specific lab conditions. Real-world may differ.
