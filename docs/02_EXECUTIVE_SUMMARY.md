# Executive Summary

## The Problem

Centralized Identity and Access Management (IAM) systems are single points of failure. Government and defense organizations face data breaches, opaque access logs, insider threats, and lack of cryptographic auditability.

Additionally, digital documents, classified materials, exam content, and training videos are vulnerable to unauthorized capture via cameras and screen recording.

## The Solution

CypherID delivers two integrated capabilities:

### 1. Blockchain IAM Platform
- Self-sovereign identity via DIDs on Hyperledger Fabric
- On-chain RBAC + ABAC access control with immutable audit trail
- Tokenized digital assets (documents, licenses, IP) with encrypted IPFS storage
- AI anomaly detection via Python/Kafka microservice

### 2. Camera-Resistant Protected Content
- Software-based rendering engine designed to reduce camera capture readability
- Dynamic session-specific watermarking for forensic traceability
- Protected modes for documents, exams, and video
- Layered browser-side capture monitoring
- Camera Resistance Lab for empirical evaluation
